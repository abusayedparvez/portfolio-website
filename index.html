About Me

I am ABU SAYED PARVEZ, a Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) student at Pabna University of Science and Technology (PUST), Bangladesh. My academic interests include pharmaceutical sciences, natural product research, computational drug discovery, and pharmaceutical analysis. I am passionate about applying scientific research to improve healthcare and promote evidence-based medicine.

Beyond academics, I create educational content on Pharmacy, Health, Science, Higher Education, and personal development to make complex topics easier to understand and more accessible for students and lifelong learners.

During my university years, I served as General Secretary of Arshinagar (PUST), Vice President of Jonaki (PUST), and Joint General Secretary of the Pharmacy Association (PUST). These experiences strengthened my leadership, teamwork, communication, and organizational skills while working with diverse groups of people.

Research & Projects

Bachelor's Research Project

Medicinal Plant-Based Drug Discovery

Department of Pharmacy
Pabna University of Science and Technology (PUST)

Phytochemical Characterization, Evaluation of Antioxidant, Antibacterial, Cytotoxic and Thrombolytic Activities, and in silico Analysis of the n-Hexane Fraction of Syzygium fruticosum Leaves.

This undergraduate research investigated the pharmacological potential of Syzygium fruticosum leaves through phytochemical characterization, in vitro biological assays, and computational drug discovery approaches. The study integrated FTIR spectroscopy, GC-MS profiling, molecular docking, and SwissADME analysis to identify promising bioactive compounds with potential therapeutic applications.
